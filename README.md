## The joke

> The printer, the Wi-Fi, and my motivation all broke at 9 a.m. — IT fixed the first two.

87 characters, one line. Written for the judge's brief: a genuine office joke
(the 9 a.m. printer-and-Wi-Fi outage every office worker knows), a real turn
at the end (the punchline lands on the third item of the list — motivation —
which IT can never fix), tight wording (every word earns its place), and a
fresh take (motivation as office equipment that breaks, rather than the usual
Monday-morning line).
